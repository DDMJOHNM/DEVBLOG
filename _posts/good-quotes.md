## AI

> The combination is what matters. This is everything that makes humans truly intelligent: abstract reasoning, logical thinking, creative vision, and judgment. Not "I can grind code for 8 hours." AI handles the grind now. But someone needs to think at a level where they can direct it, constrain it, and catch its mistakes.
>
> Google's Addy Osmani calls it the "70% problem" — non-engineers hit a wall because they can't guide AI correctly. AI doesn't replace engineering judgment. It exposes who actually has it.

## Problem Solving

> Comfort with ambiguity: You make progress even when requirements evolve. You break down complex problems, make sensible trade-offs, and seek clarity at the right moments.

## Job Search

> In addition to standard job boards like SEEK and Trade Me, effective job search mechanisms for the tech industry in Auckland, NZ, include specialist recruitment agencies, direct applications to company websites, and professional networking through events and social media. Up to 30% of tech roles are not publicly advertised on general boards. Also, it's now about visibility.
