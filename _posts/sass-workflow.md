Product and engineering teams are deep in the New SaaS shift — agentic development, vibe-coding workflows, bigger bets, usage-value pricing, tighter security posture, and portfolios instead of products. It’s exciting. It’s uncomfortable. It’s happening fast.

At SaaSpocalypse Now 2026, the Product & Engineering stream digs into:

- **Agentic development** — how far along are we, really?
- **Overlapping roles** — who does what now?
- **Bigger bets** — and how do we make them more often?
- **Modern software delivery** — what’s actually changed?
- **Portfolios over products** — are we already there?
- **Security posture** — what we’re doing, and what we’re not
- **Who’s doing it well**, and what tomorrow looks like
